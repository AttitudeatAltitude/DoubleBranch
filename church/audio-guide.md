# Sermon Audio Recording Guide

REFERENCE — written for whoever runs the soundboard. Draft; test it on
one Sunday before relying on it. Exact button names: check the Zoom
LiveTrak L-20 Operation Manual (free PDF on zoomcorp.com) — this guide
gives the steps, the manual gives the buttons.

## Why this works with no budget

The church's soundboard, a **Zoom LiveTrak L-20**, is also a recorder.
It saves recordings straight to an SD card — up to 20 channels plus the
stereo mix, as WAV files. No computer needed at the board. The only
possible cost is an SD card if the board doesn't already have one.

**What you need**
- An SD card: SDHC 4–32 GB or SDXC 64 GB+, Class 10 or faster (about
  $10 if the church needs one). A 32 GB card holds many weeks of
  stereo-only recordings.
- A computer or phone at home with an SD card reader, to copy the file
  off after the service.

## One-time setup

1. Insert the SD card. Format it **in the L-20** (not on a computer) —
   this erases the card.
2. Set the recording format to **48 kHz, 16-bit** (plenty for speech;
   smaller files). Avoid 96 kHz.
3. Set the recorder to record **the stereo mix (master)** — that's the
   one file we'll use. Recording individual tracks too is fine but fills
   the card faster.
4. Do one test recording during a practice or a Wednesday service and
   listen back at home before the first real Sunday.

## Every Sunday

1. **Before the sermon:** check the card is in and has space.
2. **When the sermon starts:** press Record. Note the time.
3. **Watch the pulpit mic level** — it should move well into the meter
   but never hit the red. If it clips, turn the channel's gain down a
   little, not the fader.
4. **When the sermon ends:** press Stop. Don't pull the card or power
   off while it's still saving.
5. Leave the card in the board, or take it home if you're the one
   uploading (put it back before next Sunday).

Recording just the sermon (not the whole service) keeps files short and
avoids music licensing questions for songs played in the service.

## After the service (at home)

1. Copy the WAV file from the card to your computer.
2. Open it in **Audacity** (free, audacityteam.org). Trim the start and
   end, use *Effect → Normalize* so the volume is consistent week to
   week, and export as **MP3** (about 64–96 kbps mono for speech).
3. Name it `YYYY-MM-DD Title – Passage.mp3`, e.g.
   `2026-10-25 Strong and Courageous – Joshua 1.mp3`.
4. Upload it to wherever the church decides sermons will live (open
   decision D7 in `project/decisions-needed.md`).
5. Delete old recordings from the SD card once they're uploaded and
   checked.

## Common problems

- **Recording is silent or very quiet:** the recorder was set to
  record the wrong source — check it's the stereo mix.
- **Distorted / crackly:** pulpit mic gain too high. Turn gain down.
- **Room sounds echoey, voice is thin:** normal for a board mix of a
  small room; Normalize in Audacity helps. Don't add a room mic yet.
- **Card full or error:** format the card in the L-20 (after copying
  anything you need off it).
