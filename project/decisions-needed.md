## D3 — Online giving provider — leadership (and whoever handles
finances)
No provider today. Leadership is comparing options, including Venmo
(per Kevin, 2026-11-08). Claude's comparison (2026-11-08, one-page
PDF to Kevin; vendor sites and 2026 reviews, not checked on live
pricing pages):
- Venmo charity profile: 1.9% + $0.10; needs verified 501(c)(3) via
  PayPal (often the IRS letter — many small churches have none);
  weak giving statements. Personal Venmo: never.
- Tithe.ly: $0/mo; card 2.9% + $0.30, ACH 1% + $0.30; donor can
  cover fee; statements.
- Givelify: $0/mo; card 2.9% + $0.30; no ACH; statements.
- Zeffy: no fees; funded by optional donor tips; receipts.
All but Venmo need the church's EIN and a bank account in its name.
Recommendation: first confirm EIN + church bank account; then Zeffy
(lowest cost) or Tithe.ly (most church-familiar); Venmo only as a
charity profile. Fallback until chosen: give in person or by mail.
