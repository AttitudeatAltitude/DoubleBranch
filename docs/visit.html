Get directions

Watch for the bend in the road:

  • Heading north: the church is on your left, just around the bend.
  • Heading west: the church is on your right, just before the bend.

Common questions

What should I wear?
Wear whatever you like. Scripture warns against giving the best seat to the best-dressed person in the room (James 2:1–4). Whatever you wear, you're welcome here.
How long is the service?
The 11:00 worship service runs about an hour.
What is the music like?
A mix of classic hymns sung with piano, and newer worship songs sung along with recorded music.
Is there something for my kids?
Yes. We have a nursery and a children's class. [TBD: ages, and when each meets]
Where do I park and which door do I use?
Parking and the entrance are easy to find when you arrive.
Is the building accessible?
Yes. There are no stairs, so the building is wheelchair accessible.
